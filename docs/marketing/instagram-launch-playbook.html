How to Use This Playbook

This is a complete, sequenced launch plan. Work top to bottom: Phase 0 (foundations, ~1 week), Phase 1 (teaser week), Phase 2 (launch day, hour by hour), Phase 3 (the first 30 days). Every post has ready-to-paste caption text. Items marked SITE must be done in the store admin before the related post goes out.

The one strategy everything serves

HERENCIA sells trust before it sells perfume. Nobody in Egypt orders a full bottle from an unknown Instagram brand — but almost anyone will risk EGP 60 on a 2ml sample, cash on delivery, with the sample price credited back when they buy the bottle. Every post funnels to one of two actions:

  1. Order samples (low-risk first purchase → herencia-eg.com)
  2. Take the scent quiz ("Find Your Scent" → personal recommendation → sample)

Brand voice on Instagram

  • Tone: warm, assured, unhurried. An heirloom house, not a hype drop. No "🔥🔥 SALE!!".
  • Language: English captions (matches the site). Reply to DMs in whatever language the customer uses — Egyptian Arabic replies build enormous trust.
  • Look: cream / deep maroon / gold. Marble, silk, raw materials (oud wood, saffron, amber), the crest. Warm window light. Never white-background e-commerce shots.
  • Words we use: composed, matured, small batches, heirloom, ritual, the maison. Words we never use: cheap, copy, inspired-by, discount blast language.

Blocker: the current product images on the site include stock photos and a collage watermarked "YSL" — these must never appear on Instagram and must be replaced on the site before launch. One afternoon with a phone produces everything below.

  • Writing the order number on the receipt

Launch-day rules

Week 2 — "The craft"

  • Reel: "Why we make you wait" — maturation explained in 15 s (patience = quality). This is the brand's signature story; pin it to the profile.
  • Carousel: Top/Heart/Base notes explained simply, using your quiz families (Woody / Floral / Amber / Fresh).

Week 3 — "Proof week"

  • Post every review screenshot collected so far (carousel). Ask every delivered customer via WhatsApp: "Would you leave a review on the site? It appears on our homepage." (Approved 4★+ reviews automatically show on the site's testimonial carousel — mention that.)
  • Reel: customer unboxing (repost with permission) or film a friend's genuine first-sniff reaction.
  • Story ritual (permanent from now): every order packed = one story frame with the governorate: "Off to Alexandria 📦". Social proof + geography = trust.

Week 4 — "Second offer"

  • SITE Create code SAMPLES2 or run "free shipping weekend" (lower the threshold in Admin → Home for 48h).
  • Reel: "Which HERENCIA are you?" — map the 4 families to 4 personalities. Most shareable format in the niche.
  • Carousel: month recap — "31 days, X orders, Y governorates, thank you."

Micro-influencer seeding (start Week 2)

  1. List 15 Egyptian micro-creators (5k–50k followers): perfume reviewers, male grooming, lifestyle "affordable luxury". Engagement > follower count.
  2. DM script: "Hi [name] — we're HERENCIA, a new Egyptian heritage perfume house. We'd love to send you our sample collection as a gift, no strings. If you enjoy them, a story mention would make our month."
  3. Send 10 kits (full sample set + gift note in the maroon box). Expect 4–6 to post. Repost everything to Stories/REVIEWS highlight.
  4. Whoever converts followers best → offer a personal code (their name, 10%) via Admin → Discounts, and track its uses there.
