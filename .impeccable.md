# Impeccable — Project Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Egyptian perfume lovers shopping on mobile (COD-first market), gift buyers, and
scent explorers who try 2ml samples before committing to a bottle. They browse
in the evening, often on mid-range Android devices — performance budgets are real.

### Brand Personality
Heritage, patience, craftsmanship. "Luxury in every drop" — an heirloom
perfumery, not a hype brand. Voice: warm, assured, unhurried. Emotional goal:
quiet confidence and desire, never urgency or noise.

### Aesthetic Direction
Locked and implemented (see `docs/09_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, `/identity`):
cream/parchment + maroon + gold; Cinzel display over Jost body; gold hairline
rules and the ✦ divider; sparing ornamentation — premium, not busy. Light AND
dark themes (CSS vars on `data-theme`). Anti-references: mass-market flash
sales, neon/dark-glow "AI" aesthetics, cluttered marketplaces.

### Design Principles
1. Brand fidelity to `/identity` is a non-negotiable (CLAUDE.md) — do not re-pick fonts or palette.
2. Motion is scent-like: slow, drifting, dissolving. Transforms/opacity only, reduced-motion safe, zero CLS.
3. Mobile-first; Lighthouse ≥ 90 on mobile is a hard budget — no animation is worth a dropped frame.
4. One signature moment per page; everything else is quiet support.
5. Ornament must mean something (gold dust = precious materials, wisps = fragrance) — never decoration for its own sake.
