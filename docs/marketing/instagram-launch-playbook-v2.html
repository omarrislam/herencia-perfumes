How to Use v2

This edition turns the v1 strategy into a literal calendar. Dates are anchored to a launch on Thursday, 23 July 2026 — if you launch another day, shift every date by the same amount but keep the weekdays and clock times (they're chosen for Egyptian Instagram traffic: lunch 13:00, prime 20:00–21:30, and Thursday/Friday = weekend energy).

The Stitch style block — append to EVERY image prompt

Each post below has its own specific prompt. To keep the whole grid looking like one brand, paste this after every prompt:

STYLE: luxury heritage perfumery editorial photography. Palette: deep espresso brown (#241111), rich maroon (#4B1D1D), antique gold (#C29A5B), warm cream (#F5EBC6). Warm directional window light with soft shadows, marble or dark aged-wood surfaces, silk fabric accents. Cinematic shallow depth of field, photorealistic, high detail. Moody, quiet, expensive. No text, no logos, no watermarks, no people's faces. Portrait 4:5.

AI images vs. real photos — the honest rule

TagMeaning
AI OKMood, materials, textures, backgrounds — Stitch output is safe to post as-is.
REAL PHOTOYour actual bottle, label, sample box, packed orders, receipts, screenshots. AI cannot reproduce your crest/label and followers WILL zoom in. Use the Stitch prompt as a lighting & composition reference, then shoot the real thing to match it.

Proof posts must never be AI. One fake-looking unboxing costs more trust than ten great posts earn. For reels, prompts describe the shot list — film with your phone.
